# Canon adjustment summary — 27 September 2026

## Authority model

- The active Canon is now explicitly PF-01 through PF-06.
- Specifications are the only subordinate authoritative document form.
- Notes, proposals, decision records, research records, reports, evidence, references, and historical material remain supporting records rather than a third authority layer.
- PF-06 is now canonical and governs Specification lifecycle and document authority.

## Registry and contracts

- PF-03 now defines five primitive Registry record kinds: entities, objects, versions, contracts, and acts.
- Grants, permissions, offices, role assignments, delegations, admissions, memberships, placements, service relationships, and store authority are contract types when institutionally meaningful.
- No parallel grant or permission primitive may diverge from recognized contracts.
- App Contract remains the root contract for application onboarding.
- Onboarding is generalized as materialization and recognition of contracts.

## Specification direction

- Removed V0-specific wording from canonical implementation guidance.
- The first needed Specifications are framed as Contracts and Onboarding, Registry Core, and Executability and Continuity.
- Language Profiles and durable security/data mechanisms are Specifications, not a separate standard-instance class.
- Candidate Specifications remain unnumbered until instantiated.

## Document-specific impact

- PF-01: small amendment; six-document Canon and two-layer authority model.
- PF-02: no substantive changes.
- PF-03: targeted amendments to Registry, Contracts, onboarding, implementation order, and documentation governance.
- PF-04: terminology aligned to Specifications-only subordinate authority.
- PF-05: one roadmap/document-authority sentence adjusted.
- PF-06: rewritten because its previous non-canonical standard-instance model directly conflicted with the new six-Canon + Specifications decision.
